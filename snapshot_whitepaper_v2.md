### **A Next-Gen 4v4 Hero Shooter & Resource Economy**

## 1. VISION: THE ULTIMATE EVOLUTION

**SNAPSHOT** is a fast-paced, stylized 4v4 team-based third-person shooter that merges the vibrant, kinetic energy of AAA shooters with a cutthroat Web3 resource economy. By deploying on **opBNB**, SNAPSHOT eliminates the "gas barrier," offering sub-cent transactions and lightning-fast finality that keeps the focus where it belongs: on the combat.

---

## 2. THE WORLD OF ARBITRA (Lore)

Centuries after the Great Collapse, the five species of **Arbitra**—the **Urshari**, **Aeonids**, **Vexis**, **Khaurans**, and **Zynni**—no longer fight total wars. Instead, they signed **The Accord**: all territorial claims and resource rights are settled in the **Proving Grounds**.

In this world, a match isn't just a game; it's survival. Winning teams secure **Kalyx Credits** (represented by $SNAPSHOT) that provide food, energy, and water for their home districts.

*"In the shadows of the Forge-Cities, the Urshari sharpen their steel, while the Aeonids watch from the Sky-Spires. Below, in the neon-lit labs of the Vexis and the sprawling warrens of the Zynni, the next generation of combatants prepares. Only the Khaurans remember the old ways of honor, but even they know: on Arbitra, you win the match, or your district starves."*

## 4. THE ARSENAL: WEAPONRY OF THE ACCORD

Combat on Arbitra is defined by **5 weapon classes** with **5 variants each** (25 total weapons), designed for "accessibility over precision."

### **1. SHOOTERS (Assault Rifles / SMGs)**
*Versatile, medium range, reliable*

**Variants:**
- **Burst Shooter** - 3-round burst, higher accuracy
- **Auto Shooter** - Full-auto spray, close-mid range
- **Precision Shooter** - Slower fire, better range/damage
- **Spray Shooter** - High fire rate, low damage, movement speed bonus
- **Heavy Shooter** - Slow, powerful, armor penetration

---

### **2. BLASTERS (Explosive Launchers)**
*AOE damage, zone control, indirect fire*

**Variants:**
- **Direct Blaster** - Fast projectile, small splash
- **Lob Blaster** - Arc trajectory, large splash
- **Rapid Blaster** - Multiple weak explosions
- **Cluster Blaster** - Splits into smaller bombs
- **Sticky Blaster** - Attach to surfaces, remote detonate

---

### **3. CHARGERS (Bows / Sniper Rifles)**
*Long range, charge-up, precision*

**Variants:**
- **Classic Charger** - Hold to charge, release to fire
- **Snap Charger** - Quick charge, less damage
- **Piercer Charger** - Penetrates enemies and shields
- **Scatter Charger** - Charge releases 3-shot spread
- **Beam Charger** - Continuous damage beam while charged

---

### **4. SLOSHERS (Arc Weapons)**
*Over-cover firing, area denial*

**Variants:**
- **Bucket Slosher** - Wide arc, short range
- **Tri-Slosher** - 3 projectiles in fan pattern
- **Sloshing Machine** - Longer range, narrower arc
- **Explosive Slosher** - AOE on impact

### **5. DUALIES (Dual Pistols)**
*Mobility, dodge-rolling, close-range*

**Variants:**

## 5. GAMEPLAY: THE COMBAT LOOP

### **Core Mechanics**

**Loadout Customization:**
- Choose 1 Character (20 options)
- Choose 1 Weapon (25 options)
- Choose 1 Tactical Ability (4-5 per species)
- Choose 1 Ultimate Ability (3-4 per species)

**Total Unique Builds:** 20 characters × 25 weapons × ~4.5 tacticals × ~3.5 ultimates = **~7,875 possible combinations**

### **Game Modes**

**Territory Control:**
- Capture and hold multiple zones
- Score accumulates over time in controlled zones
- First to 100 points or most points at 8 minutes wins

**Payload:**
- Escort objective through checkpoints
- Attackers push, defenders stop
- Overtime mechanics for close matches

**Team Deathmatch:**
- Classic elimination mode
- First to 50 eliminations or most at 10 minutes
- Respawn waves every 5 seconds

**Seasonal Events:**
- Limited-time special modes
- Unique rewards and cosmetics

### **Match Structure**
- 5-10 minute rounds
- 4v4 team composition
- Quick matchmaking for casual play
- Best of 3 or 5 for competitive
